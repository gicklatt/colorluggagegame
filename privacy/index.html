Color Luggage Sort
Privacy Policy

Advertising update · October 7, 2026

Game progress stays on your device. Advertising uses child-directed, general-audience requests for every player. Google’s ad service may process technical information; rewarded ads are optional.

Which versions this covers

This policy applies to Color Luggage Sort versions that include Google AdMob advertising. Earlier versions without advertising do not make advertising requests. Gicklatt develops the game; our privacy contact is gicklatt@gmail.com.

Your game and progress

You can play without an account. Levels, coins, supplies, preferences, and saved boards are stored on your device. We do not upload your game progress or our local gameplay diagnostics to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app or clearing its local data removes that local progress.

Children and advertising

The game is intended for a general audience, including children under 13. Every player receives the same child-directed advertising treatment. We configure Google Mobile Ads requests as child-directed, limit ad content to the G (general audience) rating, and request non-personalised ads. We do not use an age screen to offer personalised advertising to older players. These settings reduce data use; they do not mean the advertising service processes no information.

Information processed by the advertising service

Google AdMob may receive the device IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or app information and identifiers, ad requests and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, maintain service security, and prevent fraud. Our Android build removes the advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions. On iOS we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and child-directed ad requests are configured to disable IDFA use for advertising. We do not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos for advertising. Google describes the information its advertising SDK processes in its own privacy documentation.

Advertising choices and gameplay

Watching an ad for a supply is optional: when Send Back or Shuffle stock is empty, a clearly labelled Watch Ad button offers one supply after the ad service reports completion. Closing an ad early does not spend coins or supplies. You can choose not to watch and retry a level for free. Occasional interstitial ads may appear at a natural transition after completing new campaign levels. They do not appear during sorting, on launch, or when retrying a failed level. If an ad is unavailable, the transition continues. Ads require an internet connection; the core puzzle remains playable offline.

Other services and security

The advertising integration uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. No additional mediation networks, social SDKs, cloud player accounts, or developer-operated remote analytics are included in this integration. Information sent by the Google advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google and the app stores operate their services under their own terms and privacy policies; we do not control their retention periods. Your game progress and rewarded-ad deduplication receipts stay locally on the device.

Parents and privacy requests

A parent or guardian can contact gicklatt@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning this game. We do not ask players to provide a name, email address, or other account details to play. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about a child. We can explain or remove developer-held correspondence, but cannot directly erase information held by Google; requests concerning Google services should also be directed to Google.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the game’s data practices change. The version of this policy for the advertising update is dated October 7, 2026. Contact: gicklatt@gmail.com.
