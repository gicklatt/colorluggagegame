How it plays

Three taps to a takeoff

Tap a cart

Roll an empty cart up to a dock. Three matching suitcases will ride the belt and pack themselves on board.

Match the colors

Pull suitcases from the towering pile. Every cart you fill is one less dock you can use — think two carts ahead.

Catch the flight

Clear the terminal and the plane takes off in a shower of confetti. Your boarding pass is waiting.
